# Northwind Health Plan (Commercial): PA Policy

**Policy ID:** NW-RX-2207 · **Effective:** 2026-07-01 · **Last bulletin:** NW-B-2026-09 (2026-09-15; no changes to this policy)

> FICTIONAL PAYER POLICY.

## Response windows
- Pended (more info requested): submit information within **14 calendar days** of the pend date, or the request closes as denied.
- Denial: resubmission accepted within **30 days**; formal appeal within **180 days**.
- Expedited review: available on prescriber attestation of urgency (72-hour turnaround).

## Drug: Dupilumab (Dupixent®) for moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (adults)

**Initial authorization (6 months)** requires ALL of:
1. **Diagnosis:** moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis, documented by:
   - Investigator's Global Assessment (**IGA**) ≥ 3, **or**
   - Body surface area (**BSA**) involvement ≥ 10%.
2. **Prescriber:** dermatologist, allergist, or immunologist.
3. **Step therapy:** an inadequate response, intolerance, or contraindication to **both**:
   a. a medium-to-super-high-potency **topical corticosteroid** for ≥ **28 consecutive days**, **and**
   b. a **topical calcineurin inhibitor** (tacrolimus or pimecrolimus) for ≥ **28 consecutive days**.
   Trials must be documented in chart notes with **drug, start date, end date (or duration), and outcome**.
4. Not used together with another biologic or JAK inhibitor for AD.

**Step-therapy exception:** accepted with a letter of medical necessity documenting a contraindication or intolerance to the required step.

**Second denial for the same reason:** the prescriber may request a peer-to-peer within 14 days.
