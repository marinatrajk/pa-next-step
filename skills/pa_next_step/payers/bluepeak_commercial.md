# Bluepeak Health (Commercial): PA Policy

**Policy ID:** BP-SPX-0415 · **Effective:** 2026-04-01 · **Last bulletin:** BP-B-2026-08 (2026-08-20; announced a formulary review for Q1 2027, no criteria changes effective yet)

> FICTIONAL PAYER POLICY.

## Response windows
- Pended (more info requested): submit within **10 calendar days** of the pend date.
- Denial: resubmission within **30 days**; appeal within **180 days**.
- Expedited review: prescriber attestation (72-hour turnaround).

## Formulary note: adalimumab
- **Preferred:** adalimumab-adaz (biosimilar).
- **Non-preferred:** Humira® (brand adalimumab) and other adalimumab biosimilars.

## Drug: adalimumab for moderate-to-severe rheumatoid arthritis (adults)

**Initial authorization (12 months)** requires ALL of:
1. **Diagnosis:** rheumatoid arthritis, documented by a rheumatologist.
2. **Prescriber:** rheumatologist.
3. **Step therapy:** an inadequate response to **methotrexate** at ≥ 15 mg/week for ≥ **12 weeks**, **or** a documented contraindication or intolerance to methotrexate.
4. **For a non-preferred product (e.g., brand Humira):** documented **trial and failure or intolerance of the preferred biosimilar** (adalimumab-adaz), including the date and nature of the failure or reaction. A formulary exception is required.

**Continuity of care:** there is **no** continuity-of-care exemption for non-preferred adalimumab under this policy. Patients stable on brand must still meet criterion 4.

**Second denial for the same reason:** the prescriber may request a peer-to-peer within 14 days.
